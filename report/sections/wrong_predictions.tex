We examine incorrect predictions from the frozen models by comparing the
predicted winner with the recorded outcome. Within each model and feature
setting, we rank errors by confidence, defined as $\max(p,1-p)$, where $p$ is
the predicted probability of a player~1 win. We inspect the teams and ratings
for selected high-confidence errors to identify potential limitations of the
available features.

Team preview does not reveal exact movesets, held items, player decisions or
random events during a battle. Thus, even strong team-level advantages may
not lead to a win. These are possible explanations, not confirmed causes of
individual errors, because the available preview data does not describe the
full course of play.
