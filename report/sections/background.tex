\section{Background}
Competitive Pok\'emon battles involve two players who each bring a team of six
Pok\'emon. Pok\'emon Showdown is an online battle simulator; this study uses its
Generation~9 OverUsed (OU) format~\cite{showdown}. At team preview, both players
can see the opposing species, but not the full movesets, held items or chosen
abilities. Species have six base statistics and one or two types, which affect
their offensive and defensive matchups. A type advantage may make a matchup
favorable, but does not guarantee a win. Player decisions, hidden team details
and random events such as missed attacks and critical hits also influence the
outcome. Ladder rating provides an imperfect measure of player skill. These
factors motivate predicting a win probability rather than a certain winner and
comparing team-only predictions with predictions that also include ratings.
