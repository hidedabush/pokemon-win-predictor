\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare the trained classifiers against four rules that require no model
fitting. The coin-flip baseline predicts a win probability of 0.5 for every
battle. The other baselines favor the team with greater mean base stat total,
the team with greater super-effective coverage, or the player with higher
ladder rating. For each rule, a positive feature difference gives player~1
probability 0.6, a negative difference gives 0.4, and a tie gives 0.5. These
non-extreme probabilities avoid excessive penalties for confident errors.
The shared evaluation function classifies probabilities of at least 0.5 as a
player~1 win and reports accuracy, log loss and Brier score. The rating baseline
serves as a separate skill-based comparison, not as a teams-only predictor.
